Solar Knowledge Center

Understand How Solar Works & Saves Money

Educating our clients is our core philosophy. Here is everything you need to know about Net Metering and solar system configurations.

How Solar Net-Metering Works (4 Steps)

Turn your roof into an active micro-powerhouse in seamless synergy with the electricity grid.

1

Sunlight to Clean DC Electricity

High-efficiency silicon solar cells absorb photons and generate Direct Current (DC) power during daylight.

2

Inverter AC Conversion

A smart solar inverter instantly converts DC into 230V/415V AC electricity, synchronous with internal power.

3

Self-Consumption First

Your lights, fans, and appliances consume free solar power first, reducing grid power consumption to zero.

4

Bi-Directional Net Meter Export

Surplus units automatically export to the MSEDCL grid, banking credits to offset nighttime electricity consumption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about solar energy costs, government subsidy disbursement, net metering paperwork, and plant lifespan.

For residential rooftop installations, the central government provides a direct bank transfer (DBT) subsidy of ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, and a maximum of ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or higher capacity. SwayamUrja assists with end-to-end portal registration and documentation.
Solar photovoltaic modules continue generating power using ambient diffused sunlight even on overcast and rainy days (typically generating 25% to 40% of their peak summer output). At night, your facility draws electricity smoothly from the MSEDCL grid via your net meter.
Our Tier-1 solar modules come with a 25-year linear performance warranty guaranteeing over 80-84% generation output at Year 25. String inverters come with 5 to 10-year manufacturer replacement warranties.
As a standard rule of thumb, each 1 kW of modern mono-crystalline solar panels requires approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop space. Therefore, a 3 kW system needs ~250-300 sq.ft, while a 5 kW system requires ~450-500 sq.ft.
